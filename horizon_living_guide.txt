# Horizon Living – UI/UX Guide

## Design Vision
Create an immersive community discovery experience, not a traditional real-estate portal.

## Avoid
- Top navigation bar
- Standard hero banner
- Property grid homepage
- Marketplace-style listing pages
- Generic real estate templates

## Use Instead
- Floating bottom navigation dock
- Fullscreen storytelling sections
- Scroll-driven experiences
- Community-focused exploration
- Interactive comparisons
- Premium animations

## Color Palette
Background: #F7F4EF
Primary: #18181B
Secondary: #52525B

## Typography
Headings: Space Grotesk
Subheadings: Cormorant Garamond
Body: Inter

## Sections

### 1. Intro Experience
Fullscreen video/image.
No navbar.

### 2. Floating Navigation
Bottom-center glass dock.
Explore, Communities, Lifestyle, Investment, Compare, Book Tour.

### 3. Community Explorer
One community per screen.
Scroll to reveal next community.

### 4. Interactive District Discovery
Map + animated district details.

### 5. Lifestyle Experience
Horizontal scrolling showcase.

### 6. Smart Living
Animated feature cards.

### 7. Residence Showcase
Fullscreen residence presentations.

### 8. Comparison Studio
Card-vs-card comparisons.

### 9. Financing Studio
Interactive financing calculator.

### 10. Book Tour
Calendar and appointment booking.

### 11. Customer Stories
Editorial testimonials.

### 12. Final CTA
Large immersive call-to-action.

## Animation Stack
- Framer Motion
- GSAP
- Lenis

## Effects
- Parallax
- Scroll reveal
- Sticky sections
- Horizontal scroll
- Image zoom
- Text reveal
- Smooth page transitions

## Image Style
- Contemporary architecture
- Community living
- Premium interiors
- Smart homes
- Singapore lifestyle

## Goal
Make the experience feel like Apple, Tesla, or an Awwwards-winning product website rather than a traditional property portal.
